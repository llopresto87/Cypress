### `scc-2021-914`

- **instrument:** Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021
  on standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third
  countries pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — *implementing decision*
- **provision:** the decision as a whole — the operative Articles 1–4 and the
  standard contractual clauses in the Annex
- **decision number:** (EU) 2021/914
- **adopted:** 4 June 2021 (fetched text: "of 4 June 2021"; "Done at Brussels,
  4 June 2021")
- **applies from:** entered into force on the **twentieth day following
  publication in the Official Journal** (Art. 4(1)); **Decision 2001/497/EC and
  Decision 2010/87/EU repealed with effect from 27 September 2021** (Art.
  4(2)–(3)); contracts concluded before 27 September 2021 under the old clauses
  were deemed to provide appropriate safeguards **until 27 December 2022** (Art.
  4(4)). **The OJ publication reference is `not recorded`** — the fetched
  rendering carries no OJ citation for the decision itself.
- **text_form:** **verbatim** (the decision's operative Article 1, and the
  Annex's four module titles; the full clauses text is summarised, not
  reproduced — see notes)
- **text (EN, Article 1 — the operative finding):** "1. The standard contractual
  clauses set out in the Annex are considered to provide appropriate safeguards
  within the meaning of Article 46(1) and(2)(c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 for
  the transfer by a controller or processor of personal data processed subject
  to that Regulation (data exporter) to a controller or (sub-)processor whose
  processing of the data is not subject to that Regulation (data importer). 2.
  The standard contractual clauses also set out the rights and obligations of
  controllers and processors with respect to the matters referred to in Article
  28(3) and(4) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as regards the transfer of personal
  data from a controller to a processor, or from a processor to a sub-processor."
- **text (EN, the four-module structure of the Annex, verbatim titles):**
  "MODULE ONE: Transfer controller to controller" · "MODULE TWO: Transfer
  controller to processor" · "MODULE THREE: Transfer processor to processor" ·
  "MODULE FOUR: Transfer processor to controller"

- **notes:** **Legal basis and lineage.** Adopted under GDPR **Art. 28(7) and
  Art. 46(2)(c)** (`gdpr-art-46-1-2c`); recital (24) expressly repeals the
  Directive-95/46-era Decisions 2001/497/EC and 2010/87/EU on the 27 September
  2021 timeline above, and the decision's own footnote (3) cites **Schrems II
  (C-311/18)** — the SCCs are the contractual answer to the transfer
  restrictions that judgment imposed. **Scope gate, from Art. 1(1):** the SCCs
  cover transfers **from** a controller/processor subject to the GDPR **to** a
  controller/(sub-)processor whose processing is *not* subject to it — the
  clauses are the bridge to a third-country (sub-)processor, whose DPA exercises
  the Art. 28(3) content requirements Art. 1(2) refers to. **What this entry
  does not reproduce:** the full Annex text (Clauses 1–18 — general clauses,
  data-protection safeguards, local-law effects, liability, termination,
  governing law — plus Annexes I–III) is deliberately **not transcribed**; only
  the module structure and the operative article are recorded. The full text is
  publicly available at the official_url for any pass that needs a specific
  clause verbatim. **Transfer-side navigation:** adequacy first (`gdpr-art-45`,
  `eu-us-dpf-adequacy.md`), SCCs as the Art. 46 fallback.
